One day as I was in drawing painting club, doodling with my friend, the biology (?) student next to me said something like this to her friends: "People say that life is so amazing and this means there must be a god. But I'm like *huh*? Why do we only have one throat? Like what do you mean we can just choke on our food and die?"

Life is undeniably quite a miraculous thing. A billion things had to go right for life to arise on this blue planet of ours. A million more each morning so we could wake up. This is a fact we could all be more grateful for. But what my peer said that day was also true. Evolution has indeed only given us one eosophogus, crossed the wiring of our eyes, made us the only species tall and bipedal enough to kill ourselves by falling backwards, and not given us wings (but thankfully the ability to process energy drinks).
